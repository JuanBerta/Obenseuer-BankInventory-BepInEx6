=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (summary) ===
A bank you can open anywhere with infinite, multi-page, searchable storage. Take crafting ingredients for as many crafts as you want straight from the bank and send leftovers back, and pay tenement upgrades with bank materials.

=== FILE DESCRIPTIONS ===
Obenseuer-BankInventory-BepInEx6-v1.6.1.rar : Bank Inventory 1.6.1 for BepInEx 6. Extract into the Obenseuer folder.

=== CHANGELOG (Nexus "Changelogs" tab) ===
1.6.1
- Type the amount: click the amount box (x1) on the crafting bar, type a number and press Enter (or click elsewhere); Esc cancels. Game keys are ignored while you type.
- The amount is checked: more than your ingredients allow is set to the most you can make; 0, negative numbers and text keep the old amount; the most is 999. A short note under the box says what happened.
1.6.0
- New: search any storage (backpack, crates, fridges...). Matching slots are outlined and the rest dimmed; the bank still filters across all its pages.
1.5.0

- Diegetic UI support: with Diegetic UI installed, the search box and the crafting/bank buttons use its paper look.
1.4.0

- New "to bank" button: sends a station's items, or the blueprint materials you carry, back to the bank.
1.3.0
- Tenement upgrades and renovations can use materials from the bank. Tenement storages and your inventory are used first; the bank covers the rest, and only that part is taken from it. The upgrade window counts bank items too.

1.2.3
- Fixed "Take ingredients from bank" never finding items in the bank.
1.2.2
- The ingredients button now uses the recipe you selected (not the last one you hovered) and names it.
1.2.1
- Opening the backpack (key 3) or another storage while the bank is open no longer makes the panel overflow the frame.
1.2.0
- New: "Take ingredients from bank" button at crafting stations (moves only the missing ingredients into the station) and furniture workbenches (moves the missing materials into your inventory).
- Optional AutoFillOnRecipe and UseStolenItems settings.

=== FULL DESCRIPTION (BBCode) ===
[size=5][b]Bank Inventory[/b][/size]
[i]A bank you can open anywhere, with infinite storage that is saved with your game.[/i]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Open anywhere[/b] with [b]O[/b] (configurable). Deposit by dragging items in, withdraw with drag or Take All.
[*][b]Infinite storage[/b]: starts with 30 slots and grows by one page whenever it gets full, so nothing ever drops on the floor.
[*][b]Search[/b]: a search box at the top of the screen filters the bank to the matching stacks from every page, by item name, the liquid inside, or category ("food", "tool"...).
[*][b]Search any storage[/b]: the backpack, crates, fridges and other containers get the same search box. Matching slots are outlined and the rest dimmed.
[*][b]Pages[/b]: 88 slots per page, switch with [b]Shift + ][/b] and [b]Shift + [[/b].
[*][b]STACK button[/b]: moves matching items from your main inventory into the bank (tops up existing stacks first). Never touches your backpack or equipment.
[*][b]Take ingredients from the bank[/b]: at any crafting station, select a recipe and press the button at the top of the screen. Pick how many with [b]-[/b], [b]+[/b] (Shift = 10) or [b]Max[/b] (as many as the station and bank can cover), and only the missing ingredients move from the bank into the station, using the game's own recipe matching (item groups, liquids, amounts). At a furniture workbench, the missing materials move into your inventory.
[*][b]Back to the bank[/b]: one click sends everything left in a station (spare ingredients, finished items) or a blueprint's materials you carry back into the bank.
[*][b]Tenement upgrades from the bank[/b]: upgrades and renovations count what's in the bank. Your tenement storages and inventory are used first, and only the missing part is taken from the bank.
[*][b]Saved globally[/b]: the bank survives scene changes, save/load and autosaves.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Works with [b]Craft From Storage[/b]: if its nearby chests cover an upgrade it pays as usual, otherwise the bank fills the gap.
[*]Works with [b]Diegetic UI[/b]: the search box and crafting buttons switch to its paper look automatically.
[*]Works with [b]Inventory Tweaks[/b] (you can hide this mod's STACK button with StackButton = false).
[/list]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
[code]BepInEx\config\com.juanberta.obenseuer.bankinventory.cfg[/code]
[list]
[*][b]General[/b]: OpenBankKey, NextPageKey, PreviousPageKey, StackButton
[*][b]Storage[/b]: BaseCapacity, PageSize
[*][b]Crafting[/b]: TakeIngredientsButton, AutoFillOnRecipe (fill automatically when you pick a recipe), UseStolenItems
[*][b]Tenement[/b]: UseBankForUpgrades
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [b]BepInEx 6[/b] (be.697 or newer, x64). New versions are BepInEx 6 only; the last BepInEx 5 version (1.6.0) stays in the archived files.
[*]Extract the archive into your Obenseuer folder (where Obenseuer.exe is).
[*]Start the game and press O.
[/list]
Updating: replace the DLL. Your bank contents are kept.
