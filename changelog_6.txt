Obenseuer BankInventory - Changelog (BepInEx 6 flavour)
=======================================================

v1.6.1 (2026-11-13)
--------------------
- Type the amount: click the amount box on the crafting bar at stations and workbenches, type a number, Enter or click away to set it, Esc to cancel. Game keys are muted while typing.
- Checks: capped at what your ingredients allow and at 999; 0, negative numbers and text keep the previous amount, with a short note under the box.

v1.6.0 (2026-11-11)
--------------------
- Search any storage: the backpack, crates, fridges and other containers get the same search box as the bank. Matching slots are outlined, the rest dimmed, with a found count (display only, nothing moves). It clears when you open another storage.

v1.5.0 (2026-11-11)
--------------------
- Search the bank: while the bank is open, a search box at the top of the screen filters it to the matching stacks from every page (by item name, the liquid inside, or category). The header shows how many were found. Esc or the x clears it. Game and mod hotkeys are muted while you type.
- With Diegetic UI installed (its BankInventoryPaperLook option on), the bank search box and the crafting/bank buttons use its paper look. Without it, nothing changes.

v1.4.0 (2026-11-11)
--------------------
- Choose how many to gather: the bank bar at a crafting station or furniture workbench has -, + (Shift = 10 at a time) and Max, where Max is how many crafts (or pieces of furniture) the station/inventory plus the bank can cover. Tools needed by a recipe are only gathered once.
- New "to bank" button: at a crafting station it moves everything in the station's slots (leftover ingredients and finished items) into the bank; at a furniture workbench it moves the blueprint's materials from your inventory into the bank. The bank grows a page if it needs room.

v1.3.0 (2026-11-11)
--------------------
- Tenement upgrades and renovations can use materials from the bank. The game still uses the tenement storages and your inventory first; the bank covers whatever is missing, and only that part is taken from it. The upgrade window counts bank items too.
- New config: [Tenement] UseBankForUpgrades (default on).
- Works alongside Craft From Storage: when its nearby chests cover the cost it pays as usual, otherwise the bank fills the gap.

v1.2.3 (2026-11-11)
--------------------
- Fixed "Take ingredients from bank" never finding anything in the bank (a number precision bug made every bank stack look useless), so the button now really moves the missing ingredients into the station.

v1.2.2 (2026-11-11)
--------------------
- Fixed "Take ingredients from bank" saying the bank had none of the ingredients: it was checking the last recipe you hovered, not the one you meant. It now uses the recipe the info panel shows (or the last one you hovered/picked at that same station) and the button names the recipe.

v1.2.1 (2026-11-11)
--------------------
Fixes
  * Opening the backpack (its hotkey) or another storage while the bank is open no
    longer leaves the bank's slots in the panel, which made it overflow the frame.

v1.2.0 (2026-11-11)
--------------------
New
  * Take ingredients from the bank. While a crafting station is open with a recipe
    shown, a "Take ingredients from bank" button at the top of the screen moves only
    the missing ingredients from the bank into the station's input slots. The game's
    own recipe matching decides what counts (item groups, liquids in containers,
    ammo), and the recipe amount you picked is respected.
  * At a furniture workbench the button moves the blueprint's missing materials
    from the bank into your inventory.
  * The items really move, so the game and other crafting mods (such as Craft From
    Storage) craft as usual. Nothing is taken that the recipe doesn't need.
  * Optional AutoFillOnRecipe (off by default): fill automatically when you pick a
    recipe or change the amount. UseStolenItems (off by default) also uses items
    owned by someone else. TakeIngredientsButton turns the feature off.
  * The Backpack item (14930) is never moved.

v1.1.0 (2026-10-27)
--------------------
Fixes
  * The mod no longer touches the expensive in-game Backpack item (itemId 14930)
    anywhere in its move/stack paths. Stacking is now sourced strictly from the
    player's main inventory (Inventory.instance.Slots), so backpack slots and
    character equipment can never be moved, auto-created or duplicated by the mod.
  * Kept from 1.0.x: [ / ] page navigation fix (AltGr/layout-safe key detection),
    false "bank full" on item return, Take All page overflow refresh and the
    put-back item-reference fix.

Stack button (reworked)
  * "STACK" now follows the two-pass idea of LOMS' Inventory Tweaks
    (https://github.com/LOMS/obenseuer-inventory-tweaks), reimplemented for the
    multi-page bank:
      1) top up existing stacks of the same kind (item id + liquid id),
      2) fill free slots through the vanilla AddItem path (same checks as a
         drag & drop, nothing is ever dropped).
  * The vanilla move path (SlotController.AddItem) handles category/stack-size
    rules, so it stays compatible with other mods and the base game.

Credits
  * Thanks to LOMS for the obenseuer-inventory-tweaks mod - the stacking
    implementation was inspired by its approach.
